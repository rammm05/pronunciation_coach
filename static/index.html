FAQ

Good to know

Does it judge my accent?

No. Your accent is never treated as an error. The coach checks whether your words were understood, and points out specific sound swaps you can practise.

Is my data private?

Your 30-day history lives in your browser's local storage on this device, and older sessions are deleted automatically. Chrome's speech service turns your voice into text. When the Groq backend is connected, the recording or transcript is sent to it for analysis.

Do I need to pay?

No. The project is free to use.

Which browser works best?

Chrome or Edge, with microphone permission allowed. Other browsers show a demo result that is not saved.

Will I lose my history if I clear browser data?

Yes — it is stored only on this device. Use Export JSON / CSV in the history section to keep a copy.
